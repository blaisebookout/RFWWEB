App Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Built for beginners. Tough enough for fighters.

🎙️

AI Voice Coaching

Choose your coach — John or Samantha. Real-time audio cues guide every interval so you never have to glance at your phone mid-run.

📍

GPS Route Tracking

See your route come alive on a live map as you run. Full route playback and a shareable run summary when you finish.

⏱️

Smart Interval Timer

Color-coded phase bars show exactly where you are in each workout — warm-up, run, walk, cooldown — at a glance.

🏅

Achievement Badges

Earn six milestone badges as you progress from First Step through Win Your Race. Each badge celebrates a real achievement.

📖

Weekly Devotions

Each week unlocks a new devotion to keep you mentally strong — because finishing a 5K is as much about your mind as your legs.

🎵

Music Integration

Connect Apple Music, Spotify, YouTube Music, or Pandora. Audio ducks automatically when your coach speaks so you never miss a cue.

🌧️

Live Weather Radar

Check rain radar right inside the map view before you head out. No need to switch apps to decide if it's safe to run.

↩️

Turn Back Options

Set a time-based or distance-based turn-back alert so you never end up too far from home at the end of a run. Pro feature.

📤

Run Sharing

Snap a post-run selfie, overlay your stats on one of five gradient backgrounds, and share straight to your social feed.

FAQ

Common questions.

Not at all. Run Fight Win is built from the ground up for complete beginners. Week 1 starts with short 60-second run intervals broken up by walking rest periods. The program gradually builds your endurance every week so your body has time to adapt without injury.
Yes. Week 1 is fully unlocked for everyone. The complete 8-week Couch to 5K program is available on the free Starter tier. Pro and Champion subscriptions add premium features like bonus themed runs, advanced stats, devotions, and more — but you can absolutely run your first 5K without spending a dime.
Run Fight Win requires iOS 17 or later. It works on iPhone models that support iOS 17, which includes iPhone XS and newer. GPS tracking and live map features require a physical device — they won't work in the iOS Simulator.
Yes. Run Fight Win integrates with Apple Music, Spotify, YouTube Music, and Pandora. Your music plays in the background and automatically ducks (lowers volume) whenever your AI coach delivers a voice cue, then returns to full volume when the cue finishes.
Bonus Runs are themed interval workouts outside the main 8-week program — think Lightning Runs, Color Runs, and Nature Runs. They're a fun way to shake up your training routine and keep things fresh once you've built a solid base. Pro subscribers get unlimited access to all bonus run themes, including new ones as they're added.
Turn Back Options is a Pro safety feature that alerts you when it's time to head back home. You can set a time-based alert (e.g., turn around at the halfway point of your run time) or a distance-based alert. Your AI coach will give you a voice cue, and the app will calculate the best time for you to reverse direction so you finish close to your starting point.
You can absolutely use Run Fight Win on a treadmill. The interval timer and AI voice coaching work completely independently of GPS. Route tracking and the live map will simply show no movement, but all coaching, timing, badges, and progress tracking work exactly the same indoors.
Subscriptions are managed through Apple's App Store. To cancel, go to Settings → Your Name → Subscriptions on your iPhone, find Run Fight Win, and tap Cancel Subscription. You'll retain Pro access until the end of your current billing period. We never charge cancellation fees.
