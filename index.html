App Features

Everything you need.
Nothing you don't.

Built for beginners. Tough enough for fighters.

🎙️

Voice Coaching

Professionally recorded audio cues guide every interval — warm-up, run, walk, cooldown — so you never have to glance at your phone mid-run. Your coach celebrates your wins and pushes you through the hard parts.

📍

GPS Route Tracking

Watch your route come alive on a live map as you run. Full route replay and a shareable run summary card with your stats when you finish. Export your route as a GPX file too.

⏱️

Smart Interval Timer

Color-coded phase bars show exactly where you are in each workout at a glance. Purple for warm-up, pink for run, blue for walk. Skip the cooldown when you're done and finish strong.

🏅

Achievement Badges

Earn six milestone badges as you progress — First Step, Locked In, Keep Going, Fight On, Still Here, and Well Done. Each badge marks a real moment in your journey to 5K.

📖

Weekly Devotions

Each week unlocks a fresh devotion to keep you mentally strong — because finishing a 5K is as much about your mind as your legs.

Pro Feature
🎵

Music Integration

Connect Apple Music, Spotify, YouTube Music, or Pandora. Your music automatically ducks whenever your coach speaks and returns to full volume when the cue finishes — seamlessly.

❤️

Apple Health Sync

Every run is automatically saved to Apple Health. Calories burned, distance, route — all synced so your fitness data is always in one place.

↩️

Turn Back Alerts

Set a time-based or distance-based turn-back alert so you never end up too far from home. Your coach will cue you exactly when to reverse so you finish close to your start point.

Pro Feature
📱

Live Activity & Lock Screen

Your run stats live on the iPhone Lock Screen and Dynamic Island while you run — phase timer, distance, and progress — without unlocking your phone.

🌧️

Weather Updates

Check rain radar right inside the map view before you head out. Know if conditions are safe before you lace up — no need to switch apps.

🧭

Compass View

A built-in compass helps you stay oriented on unfamiliar routes — handy when you're pushing into new neighborhoods and need to find your way back.

📤

Share Your Run

Celebrate every finish line. Overlay your stats on a gradient card and share straight to social media. Your progress deserves to be seen.
