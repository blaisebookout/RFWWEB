Do I need any running experience to start? + Not at all. Run Fight Win is built from the ground up for complete beginners. Week 1 starts with short run intervals broken up by walking rest periods. The program gradually builds your endurance every week so your body has time to adapt without injury. Is Week 1 really free? What's included? + Yes — Week 1 (all 3 workouts) is completely free with no credit card required. You get the full experience: voice coaching, GPS tracking, Apple Health sync, music integration, Live Activity widget, and achievement badges. When you're ready to continue, Pro unlocks Weeks 2–8 plus bonus runs and devotions for $2.99/month. What does Pro cost and what does it unlock? + Pro is $2.99 per month, cancel anytime through the App Store. It unlocks Weeks 2–8 of the full 8-week Couch to 5K program, weekly devotions, all three bonus run themes (Lightning, Color, and Nature), Turn Back safety alerts, GPX export, and the compass view. What iOS version and device do I need? + Run Fight Win requires iOS 17 or later. It works on iPhone XS and newer. GPS tracking and the live map require a physical device — they won't function in the iOS Simulator. Can I use my own music while running? + Yes. Run Fight Win integrates with Apple Music, Spotify, YouTube Music, and Pandora. Your music plays in the background and automatically ducks whenever your coach delivers a voice cue, then returns to full volume when the cue finishes. What are the Bonus Runs in Pro? + Bonus Runs are three themed interval workouts outside the main 8-week program — Lightning Run (high-intensity sprints), Color Run (fun and playful pacing), and Nature Walk (mindful and easy). They're a great way to stay active on rest days or shake up your routine after building a solid base. How does the Turn Back alert work? + Turn Back is a Pro safety feature that alerts you when it's time to head back. You can set a time-based alert (e.g., turn around at the halfway point of your run time) or a distance-based alert. Your coach will deliver a voice cue so you can reverse direction and finish close to your starting point. Can I run on a treadmill? + Absolutely. The interval timer and voice coaching work independently of GPS. Route tracking will simply show no movement, but all coaching, timing, badges, and progress tracking work exactly the same indoors. How do I cancel my Pro subscription? + Subscriptions are managed through Apple's App Store. Go to Settings → Your Name → Subscriptions on your iPhone, find Run Fight Win, and tap Cancel Subscription. You'll retain Pro access until the end of your current billing period. No cancellation fees, ever. SUPPORT We've got your back. Every mile of the way.
